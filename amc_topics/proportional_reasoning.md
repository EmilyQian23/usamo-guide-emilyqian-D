## Key Concepts to Study

- **Direct Proportion**: $y = kx$
- **Inverse Proportion**: $y = \frac{k}{x}$
- **Unit Rates**: Comparing quantities with different units
- **Ratio Simplification**: Finding equivalent ratios
- **Cross-multiplication**: Solving proportions $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$
- **Scaling**: Increasing/decreasing all dimensions proportionally

## Problem-Solving Strategies

1. **Identify the relationship**: Is it direct or inverse?
2. **Set up the proportion**: Write as $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$
3. **Cross multiply**: $ad = bc$
4. **Solve for unknown**: Isolate the variable
5. **Check units**: Ensure answer makes sense

## Common Pitfalls

- Inverting ratios accidentally
- Forgetting to reduce fractions
- Confusing which quantity goes in numerator vs denominator
- Not keeping track of units
